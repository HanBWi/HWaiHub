When it fits

For companies that don't want to guess.

  • You sense that time is being lost but don't know where the biggest lever is.
  • Individual employees use AI – without a shared plan and without rules.
  • You have Microsoft 365 or other software that hardly makes a difference day to day.
  • Management, IT and departments need a shared basis for decisions.
  • You want to invest – but only once benefit and effort are clear.

What you get

Five results you can keep using without us.

01

Process map

The workflows examined – how they really run today, where media breaks, waiting times and knowledge silos are.

02

Prioritised use cases

Assessed by business value, effort, data situation and risk – not by technical feasibility alone.

03

Cost-benefit

Transparent assumptions on time saved and effort for the most important use cases, with your figures instead of flat rates.

04

Implementation plan

Order, responsibilities and a proposal for the first pilot – technology-agnostic, often in the Microsoft environment you already have.

05

Rules & accountability

Data rules, permissions, human approvals and AI literacy – what should be clarified before the start.

Not included

Implementation

The analysis does not implement anything. If you want to go further, the next step is a limited AI & Automation Pilot.

How it works

Little effort for your team, a clear decision at the end.

  1. Kick-offdefine goals, areas, participants
  2. Conversationsshort, with the people in the process
  3. Assessmentuse cases, data, systems, risks
  4. Decisionresult, implementation plan, pilot proposal

We agree on scope and duration together upfront. You don't need your own project team.

How it stays under control

AI suggests. Responsibility stays with people where it matters.

  • Only data that is needed for the analysis
  • Confidentiality agreed before we start
  • Your IT provider is involved where systems are affected
  • The results belong to you

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How long does the AI Transformation Analysis take?

Usually a few weeks. We agree on scope and duration together upfront.

How much effort is involved for our team?

Little: short conversations with the people who know the processes and one joint session for prioritisation. You don't need your own project team.

Is implementation included?

No. The analysis delivers priorities, a cost-benefit calculation and an implementation plan. Implementation starts – if you wish – with an AI & Automation Pilot.

Is there funding for it?

That depends on your company and project. The funding check gives a first orientation; only the responsible funding body can confirm funding.

Planning a specific digitalisation or AI project in Germany? The funding check gives a first orientation on which funding options you should look into. Only the responsible funding body can confirm funding.
