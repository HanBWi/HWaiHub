Question 1 of 16 A · Starting point

Answer the way things actually work today – not the way they should. There are no wrong answers.

How are your core processes – enquiry, order, invoice – described today?

Automation needs a repeatable workflow. Where it is missing, the work starts with the process – not with the tool.

Where is the data your employees need every day – customers, prices, orders?
Which shared work platform does your company use?
How long does it take from a customer enquiry to a finished quote?

No classic quotes? Think of order confirmations, cost estimates or appointment confirmations.

How are documents – invoices, delivery notes, contracts – captured and passed on?
How are incoming customer enquiries handled?
How quickly do employees find internal information – prices, workflows, policies?
How are the metrics you use to steer the company produced?
How do your employees work with AI tools today?
Are there rules on which data may go into AI tools and who checks results?
What is management's attitude towards AI?

    FAQ

    Frequently asked questions

    What is the AI Potential Check?

    16 questions about processes, data, time wasters and your company's maturity. The result shows your AI maturity with a maturity profile, the lever per area, three priorities with a suitable approach and a roadmap. The recommendations take your prerequisites into account – for example whether Microsoft 365 is in place or whether workflows and data can already support automation. It is an orientation, not an audit.

    Are my answers stored?

    The evaluation runs in your browser. Data is only stored if you request your personal PDF report at the end and explicitly consent: HWaiHub then receives your contact details, answers and result so we can help you with questions.

    What do I do with the result?

    You can save it as a personal PDF report – with summary, maturity profile, priorities and roadmap –, share it with your management or discuss it in the free intro call.
