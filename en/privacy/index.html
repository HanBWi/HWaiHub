Privacy policy

This is a convenience translation. The German version is legally binding.

1. Controller

Hanna Winkelmann · HWaiHub · Rua do Alcaide 59, 2750-097 Cascais, Portugal · info@hwaihub.com

2. In short

This website uses no tracking or marketing cookies, no analytics tools, no social media plugins and does not load fonts or content from third parties. Personal data is only processed to the extent necessary to operate the website or to handle your enquiry.

3. Hosting and server log files

When you visit the website, the hosting provider processes technically necessary data (IP address, date and time, page visited, browser and operating system) in order to deliver the page and ensure security. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in secure operation).

The hosting provider is GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA. GitHub logs visitors' IP addresses for security purposes. Data may be transferred to the USA in the process. GitHub is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework (adequacy decision of the EU Commission, Art. 45 GDPR). GitHub determines how long it stores this data; details can be found in GitHub's privacy statement.

4. Fonts

The fonts used (Inter, Cormorant Garamond) are hosted on our own web server. No connection to Google servers takes place.

5. Contact form and Academy waiting list

When you use the contact form, your name, your email address, optionally your company, company size and topic, as well as your message are processed in order to answer your enquiry. For the Academy waiting list, this is your email address, optionally your name and your area of interest, in order to inform you about the start of the programme. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (pre-contractual measures) or (a) (your consent).

If you reached the website via a link with a campaign tag (e.g. from LinkedIn), this tag is sent along as an indication of origin – only if you submit the form yourself. No tracking takes place.

Form entries are forwarded via an automation service to a contact system and confirmed by email. For this we use the following processors, with each of which a data processing agreement under Art. 28 GDPR is in place:

  • Make (Celonis, Inc., USA) – forwards the form data. We use Make's EU region. Transfers to the USA take place on the basis of the EU-US Data Privacy Framework, for which Celonis, Inc. is certified, or alternatively on the basis of standard contractual clauses (Art. 46 GDPR).
  • Notion (Notion Labs, Inc., 685 Market Street, San Francisco, CA 94105, USA) – stores your enquiry in our contact system. The data is processed in the USA. Notion Labs, Inc. is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework; the standard contractual clauses contained in the data processing agreement also apply.
  • Microsoft 365 (Microsoft Ireland Operations Ltd., Dublin, Ireland) – sends the confirmation of receipt to you and the notification to us by email (see section 6 on Microsoft).

Retention period: We delete enquiries no later than 24 months after the last contact if no contract has resulted. If a contract results, the statutory retention periods apply. We delete entries on the Academy waiting list as soon as you unsubscribe, but no later than 12 months after the start of the programme. At your request, we will delete your data earlier at any time, unless a legal obligation prevents this.

6. Appointment booking

You can book intro calls via Microsoft Bookings. The booking page is only linked, not embedded: no content from Microsoft is loaded when you visit this website. Only when you click "Book an intro call" does Microsoft's booking page open in a new tab.

If you book an appointment there, the details entered (in particular name, email address, chosen appointment and your voluntary information) are processed in order to arrange the appointment, send a confirmation and provide a Microsoft Teams meeting. The data is stored in Microsoft 365 (Exchange Online); Microsoft processes it on our behalf. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (pre-contractual measures).

The contractual partner is Microsoft Ireland Operations Ltd., One Microsoft Place, South County Business Park, Leopardstown, Dublin 18, Ireland. The basis is Microsoft's data protection addendum (Microsoft Products and Services Data Protection Addendum) as a data processing agreement. Our Microsoft 365 account is assigned to the European Union/EFTA region: Exchange Online, in which the bookings are stored, as well as Teams, OneDrive and SharePoint store data in data centres in the EU or EFTA area (EU Data Boundary). Where data is transferred to the USA in individual cases, Microsoft Corporation is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework; standard contractual clauses also apply. Retention period: We delete booking data no later than 12 months after the appointment if no contract has resulted.

7. AI Potential Check and funding check

Your answers in both checks are evaluated in your browser. Nothing is transmitted in the funding check. In the AI Potential Check, you can request a personal PDF report at the end. Only if you provide your name and email address (optionally company and role) for this and expressly consent do we transmit this information together with your answers and the result to us – via the same route as the contact form (Make, Notion). Purpose: to create the report, answer your questions about the result and, if you wish, contact you. The legal basis is your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), which you can withdraw at any time with effect for the future, e.g. by email to info@hwaihub.com. The same processors as in section 5 (Make, Notion, Microsoft 365) apply to the transmission, storage and confirmation email. We delete the information immediately after your withdrawal, otherwise no later than 24 months after the last contact if no contract has resulted. A link for sharing the result only contains the encoded answers, no personal data.

8. Storage in the browser

The website only stores technically necessary information locally in your browser: your acknowledgement of the storage notice, for the duration of the session the summary of the potential check, a campaign tag from the link and a counter against multiple submissions of the form. This data does not leave your device, except as part of a message that you submit yourself. Legal basis: Section 25(2) TDDDG (German Telecommunications Digital Services Data Protection Act) or corresponding provisions (technically required).

9. Your rights

You have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection (Art. 15–21 GDPR) as well as the right to withdraw consent given at any time with effect for the future. Where we process data on the basis of legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR), you can object to this processing on grounds relating to your particular situation. To do so, write to info@hwaihub.com.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, for example the competent Portuguese authority (Comissão Nacional de Proteção de Dados, cnpd.pt) or the authority at your place of residence.

10. Changes

This policy will be updated if the website or the legal situation changes.
