Privacy policy

This is a convenience translation. The German version is legally binding.

1. Controller

Hanna Winkelmann · HWaiHub · Rua do Alcaide 59, 2750-097 Cascais, Portugal · info@hwaihub.com

2. In short

This website uses no tracking or marketing cookies, no analytics tools and no social media plugins. No fonts or content are loaded from third parties on the pages of this website; the only exception is the older English and Polish legal texts (see section 4). Personal data is only processed where there is a legal basis under Art. 6(1) GDPR: your consent (lit. a, e.g. newsletter, report from the potential check), contract or pre-contractual measures (lit. b, e.g. enquiries and appointment booking) or our legitimate interest in secure and stable operation (lit. f).

3. Hosting and server log files

When you visit the website, the hosting provider processes technically necessary data (IP address, date and time, page visited, browser and operating system) in order to deliver the page and ensure security. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in secure operation).

The hosting provider is GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA. GitHub logs visitors' IP addresses for security purposes. Data may be transferred to the USA in the process. GitHub is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework (adequacy decision of the EU Commission, Art. 45 GDPR). GitHub determines how long it stores this data; details can be found in GitHub's privacy statement.

4. Fonts

The fonts of this website (Inter, Cormorant Garamond) are hosted on our own web server. No connection to Google servers takes place.

Exception: the older English and Polish legal texts (English privacy policy and legal notice, Polish privacy policy and Nota prawna) load fonts from Google Fonts (Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland; parent company Google LLC, USA). Your browser connects to Google servers and transmits your IP address. Legal basis is our legitimate interest in a consistent presentation (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Google LLC is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. More: policies.google.com/privacy. The free-guide pages do not load Google Fonts.

5. Contact form and Academy waiting list

When you use the contact form, your name, your email address, optionally your company, company size and topic, as well as your message are processed in order to answer your enquiry. For the Academy waiting list, this is your email address, optionally your name and your area of interest, in order to inform you about the start of the programme. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (pre-contractual measures) or (a) (your consent).

If you reached the website via a link with a campaign tag (e.g. from LinkedIn), this tag is sent along as an indication of origin – only if you submit the form yourself. No tracking takes place.

Form entries are forwarded via an automation service to a contact system and confirmed by email. For this we use the following processors, with each of which a data processing agreement under Art. 28 GDPR is in place:

  • Make (Celonis, Inc., USA) – forwards the form data. We use Make's EU region. Transfers to the USA take place on the basis of the EU-US Data Privacy Framework, for which Celonis, Inc. is certified, or alternatively on the basis of standard contractual clauses (Art. 46 GDPR).
  • Notion (Notion Labs, Inc., 685 Market Street, San Francisco, CA 94105, USA) – stores your enquiry in our contact system. The data is processed in the USA. Notion Labs, Inc. is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework; the standard contractual clauses contained in the data processing agreement also apply.
  • Microsoft 365 (Microsoft Ireland Operations Ltd., Dublin, Ireland) – sends the confirmation of receipt to you and the notification to us by email (see section 6 on Microsoft).

Retention period: We delete enquiries no later than 24 months after the last contact if no contract has resulted. If a contract results, the statutory retention periods apply. We delete entries on the Academy waiting list as soon as you unsubscribe, but no later than 12 months after the start of the programme. At your request, we will delete your data earlier at any time, unless a legal obligation prevents this.

6. Appointment booking

You can book intro calls via Microsoft Bookings. The booking page is only linked, not embedded: no content from Microsoft is loaded when you visit this website. Only when you click "Book an intro call" does Microsoft's booking page open in a new tab.

If you book an appointment there, the details entered (in particular name, email address, chosen appointment and your voluntary information) are processed in order to arrange the appointment, send a confirmation and provide a Microsoft Teams meeting. The data is stored in Microsoft 365 (Exchange Online); Microsoft processes it on our behalf. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (pre-contractual measures).

The contractual partner is Microsoft Ireland Operations Ltd., One Microsoft Place, South County Business Park, Leopardstown, Dublin 18, Ireland. The basis is Microsoft's data protection addendum (Microsoft Products and Services Data Protection Addendum) as a data processing agreement. Our Microsoft 365 account is assigned to the European Union/EFTA region: Exchange Online, in which the bookings are stored, as well as Teams, OneDrive and SharePoint store data in data centres in the EU or EFTA area (EU Data Boundary). Where data is transferred to the USA in individual cases, Microsoft Corporation is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework; standard contractual clauses also apply. Retention period: We delete booking data no later than 12 months after the appointment if no contract has resulted.

7. AI Potential Check and funding check

Your answers in both checks are evaluated in your browser. Nothing is transmitted in the funding check. In the AI Potential Check, you can request a personal PDF report at the end. Only if you provide your name and email address (optionally company and role) for this and expressly consent do we transmit this information together with your answers and the result to us – via the same route as the contact form (Make, Notion). Purpose: to create the report, answer your questions about the result and, if you wish, contact you. The legal basis is your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), which you can withdraw at any time with effect for the future, e.g. by email to info@hwaihub.com. The same processors as in section 5 (Make, Notion, Microsoft 365) apply to the transmission, storage and confirmation email. We delete the information immediately after your withdrawal, otherwise no later than 24 months after the last contact if no contract has resulted. A link for sharing the result only contains the encoded answers, no personal data.

8. Storage in the browser

The website only stores technically necessary information locally in your browser: your acknowledgement of the storage notice, for the duration of the session the summary of the potential check, a campaign tag from the link and a counter against multiple submissions of the form. This data does not leave your device, except as part of a message that you submit yourself. Legal basis: Section 25(2) TDDDG (German Telecommunications Digital Services Data Protection Act) or corresponding provisions (technically required).

9. Newsletter and free guide

On the free-guide pages (hwaihub.com/prompts in German, hwaihub.com/en/prompts in English, hwaihub.com/pl/prompty in Polish) you receive our free guide "10 Profit Prompts". Where a sign-up form is offered, you can additionally subscribe to the newsletter with AI tips for self-employed people. If the page offers a direct download, no personal data is collected for it (apart from the server log files under section 3).

Which data? First name and email address and, as proof of your sign-up, the time and IP address of the sign-up and of the confirmation.

Double opt-in: After signing up you receive an email with a confirmation link. Only after you confirm are you added to the mailing list and receive the guide.

Legal basis and withdrawal: The processing is based on your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). You can unsubscribe at any time via the unsubscribe link in every email or by writing to info@hwaihub.com.

Email service MailerLite: We use MailerLite (MailerLite Limited, 88 Harcourt Street, Dublin 2, D02 DK18, Ireland) for sending. Your data is stored on MailerLite's servers. MailerLite may use sub-processors outside the EU; in that case the transfer is based on the EU Standard Contractual Clauses. A data processing agreement is in place with MailerLite. More: MailerLite Privacy Policy.

Analysis: The emails may contain technical elements that show whether an email was opened and which links were clicked. This helps us improve the content. This analysis is also based on your consent and ends when you unsubscribe.

Retention period: We store your data until you unsubscribe. Afterwards we may keep your email address on a block list so that you receive no further emails (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

10. Links to social networks

This website contains simple links to LinkedIn and Instagram. No social media plugins are loaded. Only when you click a link do you reach the respective provider, whose privacy terms then apply.

11. Instagram and automatic direct messages (Manychat)

We operate the Instagram accounts @hanna.ki.coaching (German), @hanna.ai.inpractice (English) and @hanna.aiwpraktyce (Polish). Instagram is provided by Meta Platforms Ireland Limited (Merrion Road, Dublin 4, D04 X2K5, Ireland). The processing of your data by Instagram itself is governed by Instagram's privacy policy. For the evaluation of page statistics (Insights) we are jointly responsible with Meta (Art. 26 GDPR); Meta has assumed primary responsibility (Page Controller Addendum).

Automatic replies with Manychat: If you comment a certain keyword under one of our posts (e.g. "PROFIT", "ZYSK" or "DELETE") or send us a direct message, an automated service replies to you. We use Manychat for this (Manychat, Inc., USA; contact: privacy@manychat.com). On our behalf, Manychat sends you a reply to your comment and direct messages, e.g. with the link to our free guide.

Which data? Via Instagram's interface, Manychat receives your Instagram username, display name, profile picture, an internal user ID, your comment or messages, the time of the interaction, whether you follow our account and which buttons you tapped in the messages. No email address is collected in Manychat. You can voluntarily subscribe to the newsletter on our website (see section 9).

Legal basis: The processing takes place because you request the message yourself by your comment or message (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) and on the basis of our legitimate interest in answering enquiries on Instagram quickly and reliably (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). If you no longer wish to receive automatic messages, send us a short direct message or an email to info@hwaihub.com.

Transfer to the USA and processing: Manychat processes the data on our behalf; a data processing agreement is in place with Manychat. Manychat, Inc. is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework, so an adequacy decision of the EU Commission exists for the transfer to the USA (Art. 45 GDPR). More: Manychat Privacy Policy.

Retention period: We store your contact data in Manychat for as long as you interact with our account and delete it no later than twelve months after your last interaction, or immediately if you request it.

12. Your rights

You have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection (Art. 15–21 GDPR) as well as the right to withdraw consent given at any time with effect for the future. Where we process data on the basis of legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR), you may object to this processing on grounds relating to your particular situation. Please write to info@hwaihub.com.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority. The authority responsible for us is the Portuguese one: CNPD – Comissão Nacional de Proteção de Dados, Av. D. Carlos I, 134, 1º, 1200-651 Lisbon, Portugal, www.cnpd.pt. You may also contact the supervisory authority where you live.

13. Security and changes

This website uses SSL/TLS encryption (recognisable by "https://" in the address bar). This policy will be updated if the website, the services used or the legal situation change. The version published here applies.
