For IT & data protection

What your IT and your data protection officer want to know.

Many AI projects don't fail because of technology, but because of open questions from IT and data protection. We involve both early – with clear answers instead of slides.

Typical questions from IT and data protection
Which data?We define which data may go into which tool – and which may not.
Which tools?Approved tools and contracts; private accounts are deliberately regulated.
PermissionsSharing, especially in SharePoint and Teams, is reviewed before the start.
ApprovalsHuman approval where results leave the company or affect decisions.
IT providerInvolved, not bypassed – for systems, integrations and operations.
DocumentationPolicy, training records and decisions filed traceably.

HWaiHub does not provide legal advice and does not guarantee legal compliance. We help build competence, rules and traceability; legal responsibility remains with the company. For legal questions, involve your legal adviser or data protection officer.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

May we use ChatGPT or Copilot in the company?

In principle yes – if it is clear which data may go into which tool, which contracts apply and who checks the results. For detailed legal questions, involve your data protection officer or lawyer.

What belongs in an AI policy?

Permitted tools, permissible data, approval and review rules, responsibilities, training and documentation. We develop it to fit your workflows.

Does HWaiHub guarantee compliance with the EU AI Act?

No. We help build competence, rules and traceability. Legal responsibility remains with the company; legal questions require legal advice.
