Safe use & AI literacy

Training that also counts for Article 4.

Article 4 of the EU AI Act requires companies that use AI systems to take measures that support the development of their staff's AI literacy – amended since July 2026, but still with a duty to act. Role-based, documented training can be part of these measures.

AI literacy under the EU AI Act →

Not legal advice. Which measures are sufficient overall depends on your use of AI.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Who is the Copilot training for?

For teams and managers who want to use Microsoft 365 Copilot or Copilot Chat safely and effectively in their daily work – structured by role.

Does the training count towards AI literacy under Article 4 EU AI Act?

Role-based, documented training can be part of your AI literacy measures. Which measures are sufficient overall depends on your use of AI. Not legal advice.

Is Copilot training eligible for funding?

Short workshops are generally not funded through the further-training support of the German Federal Employment Agency; it requires, among other things, more than 120 hours and an approved provider. Other funding options may be possible depending on the federal state and project – the funding check gives a first orientation.
