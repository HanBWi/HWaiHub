Explained briefly

What is Microsoft 365 Copilot – and what matters?

Microsoft 365 Copilot is an AI assistant built directly into Outlook, Teams, Word, Excel, PowerPoint and other Microsoft 365 apps. It works with the company content each person is allowed to access – emails, chats, documents.

  • Outlook
  • Teams
  • Word
  • Excel
  • PowerPoint
  • SharePoint

What this means for your company

  1. Permissions determine what Copilot finds.If a folder is shared too openly, Copilot finds it too – for everyone with access. That is why we review sharing before the start.
  2. Good filing means good answers.Outdated versions and scattered documents lead to unreliable results. Order in Teams and SharePoint pays off directly.
  3. Impact comes from the role, not the licence.Only concrete use cases per role and short training turn the licence into time saved.

As of 30.09.2026 · Features and licence terms change; we check them for your environment at the time.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is Microsoft 365 Copilot?

Microsoft 365 Copilot is an AI assistant that works in applications such as Outlook, Teams, Word, Excel and PowerPoint and can access content the respective person is authorised to see. We clarify case by case which features and licences suit your company.

What is the difference between Copilot Chat and Microsoft 365 Copilot?

Simply put: Copilot Chat is the AI chat in the Microsoft environment; Microsoft 365 Copilot also works directly in the applications and with your company content. Exact features and licence terms change – we check them for your environment at the time.

Do we need a readiness review for Copilot first?

Usually yes. Above all, permissions in SharePoint and Teams determine which content Copilot can find. A short review avoids surprises and shows which use cases make sense first.

Is Copilot always the right solution?

No. We understand the process first. Sometimes Copilot fits, sometimes Power Automate, an AI agent, another tool – or simply a better workflow.

How do you deal with ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini?

Technology-agnostic. Every tool has strengths. We recommend what fits your process, your data and your rules – without disparaging other solutions.
