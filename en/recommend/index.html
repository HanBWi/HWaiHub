Employees are often the first to see where AI could help. Here you will find everything you need to take the idea into your company – or to get in touch directly yourself.

Arguments for management

Short and factual.

  • No big project: start with a free check or intro call, then a limited pilot.
  • Measurable: metrics are set before the start and measured afterwards.
  • Existing tools: the Microsoft environment you already have is often enough.
  • Controlled: clear data rules, human approvals, IT provider involved.
  • Competence in the team: training that is also documented for Article 4 EU AI Act.
  • Transparent prices: stated bindingly before the start.
