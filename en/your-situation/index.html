Sales

Too many steps between enquiry and quote.

Enquiries, quotes and follow-ups pass through many hands. Every step waits for the next one.

Why it happens
The workflow has grown over years. Nobody owns it from enquiry to result.
What it costs
Hours per quote, missed orders, overtime in the team.

How you recognise it – and what we do about it

  1. Quotes take too long and are often written in the evening or at the weekend. SolutionQuote draft from enquiry, template and price list – a human checks and sends.
  2. Prices and customer data are spread across several sources. SolutionData is combined automatically, one valid price list.
  3. Follow-ups get lost, the CRM is out of date. SolutionReminders and CRM entries run along automatically.
  4. Calculations use old figures – orders are lost or earn too little. SolutionActual values from delivery flow back into the calculation.
  5. Only one person knows what a proper quote looks like. SolutionTemplates and rules so that a stand-in can quote safely too.
  6. More enquiries mainly mean more evenings at the office. SolutionEnquiries are pre-qualified before anyone invests time.

Administration

Receipts and data are handled several times.

Data from emails and PDFs is transferred by hand into the ERP or into lists – often several times.

Why it happens
The systems are not connected. Excel lists and emails bridge the gaps.
What it costs
Double data entry, cost of errors and a heavy load on the back office.

How you recognise it – and what we do about it

  1. Incoming invoices and receipts are captured by hand. SolutionReceipts are recognised, coding is suggested, a human checks exceptions.
  2. Documents end up unsorted in folders and mailboxes. SolutionDocuments are classified and filed in the right place.
  3. The same data is typed into several systems. SolutionSystems are connected, data is captured only once.
  4. Approvals happen informally and take time. SolutionClear approval paths with reminders and evidence.
  5. New obligations such as e-invoicing or reporting cost time. SolutionObligations are explained clearly and built into the workflow.
  6. The invoice waits until measurements and receipts have been collected. SolutionMeasurements and receipts flow directly into invoice preparation.

Customer service

Standard questions fill the inbox – there is no time left for difficult cases.

“Where is my order?” – calls and emails interrupt the actual work.

Why it happens
The information exists, but not where it is needed.
What it costs
Interruptions, escalations – and good people spend their time communicating instead of managing.

How you recognise it – and what we do about it

  1. Every morning, enquiries are sorted by hand. SolutionEnquiries are pre-sorted by topic and urgency.
  2. The same answers are written again and again. SolutionAnswer drafts from approved building blocks, a human approves.
  3. Standard questions tie up time that is missing for difficult cases. SolutionFrequent questions are answered automatically and correctly.
  4. Enquiries get lost in the inbox. SolutionNo incoming item without an owner and a deadline.
  5. Customers ask several times about the status. SolutionOne case in one place, the status visible to everyone.
  6. Cases are spread across phone, email and WhatsApp. SolutionAll channels end up in the same case.

Knowledge

Knowledge depends on a few people.

Certain questions can only be answered by one person. If they are away, the workflow stops.

Why it happens
Knowledge is scattered across folders, mailboxes and heads – without a structure that makes searching possible.
What it costs
Expensive expert time, slow onboarding, loss of knowledge when people leave.

How you recognise it – and what we do about it

  1. Information is searched for in PDFs and emails. SolutionA searchable knowledge base in your Microsoft environment.
  2. New colleagues keep asking the same things. SolutionOnboarding material and an assistant that answers questions.
  3. Policies are outdated or scattered. SolutionOne valid version, maintained and easy to find.
  4. Knowledge depends on one person – if they are on holiday, the office stops. SolutionWorkflows are documented out of heads and mailboxes.
  5. Several versions of a document – which one is valid? SolutionClean up the filing, one valid version per document.
  6. Experienced employees leave and take their knowledge with them. SolutionSecure and pass on knowledge in good time.

Management

Decisions need an up-to-date picture – not hunting for data.

Key figures sit in ERP, accounting, CRM and Excel. By the time a report is ready, they are often outdated.

Why it happens
Nobody combines the figures from the leading systems automatically.
What it costs
Days for the monthly report, decisions based on outdated figures, deviations spotted late.

How you recognise it – and what we do about it

  1. The figures are there – just not prepared. SolutionKey figures are combined automatically.
  2. The monthly report is built by hand every time. SolutionA report that prepares itself.
  3. Meetings end without recorded results. SolutionSummaries and tasks straight from the meeting.
  4. Unclear what AI, licences and Copilot credits really cost. SolutionBudget, cost limit and a monthly review per use case.
  5. The IT provider looks after systems, not workflows. SolutionWe take responsibility for the workflow between department and IT.
  6. AI agents are supposed to take over work – without clear rights and control. SolutionRights, approvals, logging and a shutdown rule before the start.

People

We already use AI – but without a shared plan.

Some use Copilot or ChatGPT for texts, others not at all. Nobody knows exactly what is allowed and what it brings.

Why it happens
Tools were introduced but not translated into roles, workflows and rules.
What it costs
Unused licences, varying quality, avoidable data protection risks.

How you recognise it – and what we do about it

  1. Copilot is there – but nobody uses it properly. SolutionRole-based use cases and training on real daily work.
  2. There is no AI policy and no evidence under Art. 4 of the EU AI Act. SolutionA one-page AI policy and training with evidence.
  3. Training is generic and does not fit daily work. SolutionTraining with your team's tasks and tools.
  4. The team uses private AI accounts without rules. SolutionApproved tools with clear data rules.
  5. New hires are on their own by day two. SolutionStructured onboarding with materials and a contact person.
  6. Fewer people, more work – management steps in itself. SolutionHand off routine work so capacity remains for what matters.

What is on businesses' minds right now · as of October 2026

It's not the actual work that weighs people down – it's everything around it.

Quotes in the evening, knowledge in individual heads, fewer people for more work – and new obligations with fixed deadlines. Surveys by German business associations show this, as does what owners and teams write publicly about their daily work.

  • 74 %of German craft businesses say their bureaucratic workload has increased over the past five years – above all due to new regulations and reporting obligations.ZDH survey 2023, 10,630 businesses ↗
  • 40 %+of small and mid-sized companies in Germany cannot fill vacancies at least in part. Across all companies it is 36 %.DIHK skilled labour report 2025/26 ↗
  • 66 %of companies rate their employees' AI literacy as low – even though 70 % already offer training.Bitkom Research 2026, 603 companies ↗

Deadlines that are creating work now

  2. from 01.01.2027Issuing e-invoices (Germany)Mandatory for companies with more than €800,000 prior-year turnover, from 2028 for all domestic B2B transactions. PDF invoices will then no longer be sufficient.Matching area → Source (German) ↗
