How we work

Process first. Then technology.

The HWaiHub method leads from the question “Where are we losing time?” to a running, safeguarded workflow. Every solution starts with the business process, is tested small and measurably and is only expanded once the benefit is proven.

What happens

Why workflows don't improve despite AI tools – and how HWaiHub changes that

AI is not the goal. A better process is.

Microsoft 365, Copilot and ERP are often already in place. Yet workflows such as enquiry → quote, receipt → booking or customer question → answer don't get any faster. The reason: everyone takes care of their part – nobody is responsible for the workflow as a whole.

1 Today: everyone owns their part

Departmentknows the workflow
IT providerlooks after the systems
Software vendorsdemonstrate features

In between lies the workflow – e.g. from enquiry to quote. Responsible for the whole: often nobody. That is why the improvement never happens.

2 With HWaiHub: one party owns the whole workflow

We take responsibility for improving exactly this workflowfrom the analysis to the pilot until your team runs it safely itself – and we coordinate department, IT provider and software vendors. Your IT provider continues to run IT operations.

3 How it becomes a concrete plan

  1. 01Problem“My people spend hours on …”
  2. 02ProcessWhich workflow causes it? Which systems are involved?
  3. 03SolutionAutomation, Copilot, agent – or deliberately no AI.
  4. 04ResultMeasurable: time, cost, lead time, error rate.
