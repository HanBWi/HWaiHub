2. Legal bases

I only process personal data where there is a legal basis under Art. 6(1) GDPR, in particular:

  • Consent (point a), e.g. for the newsletter. You can withdraw your consent at any time with effect for the future.
  • Contract or pre-contractual measures (point b), e.g. for enquiries about my services.
  • Legitimate interest (point f), e.g. in the secure and stable operation of this website.

3. Hosting and server logs

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub Inc., 88 Colin P Kelly Jr St, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA). When you visit the website, GitHub processes technically necessary access data, in particular IP address, date and time, the page accessed, referrer URL and browser and operating system information. This data is required to deliver the website (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). GitHub is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. More information: GitHub Privacy Statement.

5. Cookies and local storage

I do not use tracking or marketing cookies or any analytics tools. Only technically necessary settings are stored in your browser’s local storage (localStorage):

  • hw_cookies: your choice in the cookie notice
  • hw_lang: the selected website language
  • hw_contact_rl: time of your last contact request, to protect against spam

This information never leaves your browser. You can delete it at any time in your browser settings.

6. Contact form and email

If you write to me via the contact form or by email, I process your details (e.g. name, email address, company, message) in order to answer your enquiry (Art. 6(1)(b) or (f) GDPR).

Messages from the contact form are transmitted via the automation service Make (Celonis Inc., One World Trade Center, 87th Floor, New York, NY 10007, USA). Processing takes place in the service’s EU region. Where data is transferred to the USA, this is based on the EU Standard Contractual Clauses or the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. A data processing agreement is in place with Make.

I delete your data once your enquiry has been fully dealt with, unless statutory retention obligations apply.

7. Newsletter and free guides

On hwaihub.com/prompts (German), hwaihub.com/en/prompts (English) and hwaihub.com/pl/prompty (Polish) you can get my free guide “10 Profit Prompts”. Where a signup form is offered, you can also subscribe to my newsletter with AI tips for solopreneurs. If the page offers a direct download, no personal data is collected for it (apart from the server logs described in section 3).

What data?

First name and email address and, as proof of your signup, the time and IP address of signup and confirmation.

Double opt-in

After signing up you receive an email with a confirmation link. Only after you confirm are you added to the mailing list and receive the guide.

Legal basis and withdrawal

Processing is based on your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). You can unsubscribe at any time via the unsubscribe link in every email or by writing to info@hwaihub.com.

Email service MailerLite

I use MailerLite (MailerLite Limited, 88 Harcourt Street, Dublin 2, D02 DK18, Ireland) to send emails. Your data is stored on MailerLite servers. MailerLite may use sub-processors outside the EU; in that case, transfers are based on the EU Standard Contractual Clauses. A data processing agreement is in place with MailerLite. More: MailerLite Privacy Policy.

Analysis

The emails may contain technical elements that show whether an email was opened and which links were clicked. This helps me improve the content. This analysis is also based on your consent and ends when you unsubscribe.

Storage period

I store your data until you unsubscribe. After that, I may keep your email address on a block list to ensure you receive no further emails (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Automated replies with Manychat

If you comment a specific keyword under one of my posts (e.g. “PROFIT”) or send me a direct message, an automated service replies to you: I use Manychat (Manychat, Inc., USA; contact: privacy@manychat.com). On my behalf, Manychat sends you a reply to your comment and direct messages, e.g. with the link to my free guide.

What data?

Via the Instagram interface, Manychat receives your Instagram username, your display name, your profile picture, an internal user ID, your comment or messages, the time of the interaction, whether you follow my account and which buttons you tapped in the messages. Manychat does not collect an email address. You receive the free guide directly by direct message; you can voluntarily subscribe to the newsletter on my website (see section 7).

Legal basis

Processing takes place because you request the message yourself through your comment or message (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) and on the basis of my legitimate interest in answering enquiries on Instagram quickly and reliably (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). If you no longer wish to receive automated messages, simply send me a short direct message or an email to info@hwaihub.com.

Transfer to the USA and data processing

Manychat processes the data on my behalf; a data processing agreement is in place with Manychat. Manychat, Inc. is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework, so an adequacy decision of the EU Commission applies to the transfer to the USA (Art. 45 GDPR). More: Manychat Privacy Policy.

Storage period

I store your contact data in Manychat as long as you interact with my account and delete it no later than twelve months after your last interaction, or immediately on request.
