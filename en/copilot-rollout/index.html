Self-test

Do you need a planned rollout?

If at least one point applies, a short test with licences is usually not enough.

  • You are planning licences for more than a small test group.
  • Sharing in SharePoint and Teams has grown over years and is not documented.
  • The first licences are hardly used.
  • Management or the works council want to know what Copilot sees and what it is used for.

Two steps

From licence to real usage.

Each step ends with a result on which you base your decision.

  1. 1
    Preparation

    Before Copilot finds what it shouldn't.

    • OrganisationGoals, responsibilities and who decides on usage and rules.
    • Data & permissionsSharing in SharePoint, Teams and OneDrive: what is too open, what needs cleaning up?
    • Technical environmentLicences, applications and settings – coordinated with your IT provider.
    • Use casesWhich roles benefit first, and how do we recognise the benefit?

    ResultStatus report, risks with measures, prioritised use cases.

  2. 2
    Everyday use

    Copilot where the work happens.

    • Use cases per roleConcrete applications for sales, inside sales, project management or administration.
    • Integration into workflowsCopilot becomes part of existing processes, not an extra window.
    • Key usersContact people in the team who gather and share experience.
    • TrainingHands-on and by role – see Copilot training.

    ResultCopilot in daily work – with measured usage.

How we measure success

Usage, time, quality.

  • Active usage by role and application
  • Time saved in selected use cases – before and after
  • Quality of results from the users' perspective
  • Hurdles that call for follow-up training

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What does a Copilot rollout include?

Preparation (organisation, permissions, technical environment, use cases), everyday use (role-based use cases, integration into workflows, measuring usage) and enabling employees.

Why are permissions so important?

Copilot can only access content the respective person is authorised to see – but it finds everything that is reachable that way. That is why we review sharing in SharePoint and Teams before the start.

How do we measure whether Copilot is working?

Through active usage, time saved in selected use cases and the quality of results. We define the metrics before the start.
