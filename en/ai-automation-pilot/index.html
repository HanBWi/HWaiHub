Good first use cases

Recurring, noticeable, measurable.

A good pilot starts with a workflow that happens often, has clear rules and visibly costs time today.

Enquiries & quotes

Capture incoming enquiries, pre-sort them and prepare quote drafts – approved by your sales team.

Receipts & documents

Take over data from emails, PDFs and forms instead of retyping it – with a check before booking.

Internal knowledge search

Find answers in your own documents, with a visible source – instead of asking colleagues.

See all typical situations →

What it includes

Limited in scope, complete in accountability.

  • Target picture and metrics before the start – with a baseline measurement
  • Implementation with the right technology: Microsoft 365 Copilot, Power Automate, Copilot Studio, Make or other AI solutions
  • Key users from your team who help shape the workflow
  • Defined what AI prepares and where people check and approve
  • Coordination with your IT provider for system integrations
  • Final measurement and recommendation: roll out, adjust or stop

What we measure

Before and after – with your figures.

  • Lead time of a case
  • Time per case
  • Error and correction rate
  • Follow-up questions and rework
  • Acceptance among key users

We agree together which metrics fit before the start.

How it stays under control

AI suggests. Responsibility stays with people where it matters.

  • Human approval where it matters
  • Clear data rules – which data the AI may see
  • Sources visible, results traceable
  • Limited group of users before rollout

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is a good first use case?

A recurring workflow with noticeable time loss, clear rules and a measurable result – such as enquiries, receipts or internal search. We choose it together.

Who decides in the automated workflow?

Where it matters, a human. In the pilot we define what AI prepares and where employees check and approve.

How do we measure success?

We measure before and after – for example lead time, time per case, error rate or follow-up questions. We define the metrics before the start.

What happens after the pilot?

You decide based on measured results: roll out, adjust or deliberately stop.

Start small. Decide on measured results.
