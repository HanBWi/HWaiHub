Frequently asked questions

Short answers

We already have Copilot or ChatGPT. Why do we need HWaiHub?

Tools alone don't change a workflow. We connect them with your processes, clarify rules and responsibilities and make sure your team uses them safely.

Do you only work with Microsoft?

No. We are technology-agnostic and choose by process: Microsoft Copilot, Power Automate, AI agents, other AI solutions such as ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini – or simply a better workflow without a new tool.

Do we need our own project team?

No. We work with a few key users and deliberately keep your effort low.

What happens if AI delivers wrong results?

Where it matters, a human checks and decides. Sources stay visible, exceptions are defined before anything is automated.

Does this work with our ERP and our systems?

We start with what is already there and check interfaces early. For deep integration we work together with your IT provider.

What does the EU AI Act require of us?

Since 2 February 2025, Article 4 of the EU AI Act has required companies that use AI systems to take measures for the AI literacy of their staff. Since 27 July 2026 an amended version applies: the measures should support the development of AI literacy; a specific level for individual people does not have to be guaranteed. More in our guide to AI literacy. Not legal advice.

Is there funding for AI and digitalisation projects?

Depending on the German federal state, company size and project, funding options may be worth checking. The funding check gives a first orientation – without any funding commitment.
