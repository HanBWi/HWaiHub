How we teach

Three principles – in every training.

  1. 01

    Practical

    Practice happens on real tasks – emails, quotes, meetings, reports – not on demo examples.

  2. 02

    Technology-agnostic

    Microsoft Copilot as well as ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini. The focus is on the task, not the tool.

  3. 03

    Responsible

    Which data may go in, how to check results and where a person decides – from the very beginning.

Six topic areas

  • 01AI basics & working safely
  • 02Microsoft 365 & Copilot in everyday work
  • 03AI assistants compared
  • 04Automating workflows
  • 05Role deep dives
  • 06Leadership & AI rules
