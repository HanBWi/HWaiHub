For whom

When AI should take work off your hands.

If one of these statements applies to you, the programme is meant for you.

  • You use ChatGPT, Copilot or other assistants – but rather at random.
  • You want to do recurring tasks faster and better.
  • You are self-employed and have nobody to "introduce" AI to you.
  • You want to know what is allowed with customer data.

How you learn with us

Every course follows the same path.

First understand, then a method, then practise – and at the end know how to continue.

  1. 1WhyMindset & goal

    How AI thinks, where it goes wrong and what you want to achieve with it.

  2. 2HowMethod & prompt

    A clear method to get good answers on purpose.

  3. 3WhatTool practice

    Practise on your own tasks – with the right tool.

  4. 4Where nextLooking ahead

    Your next step: tools, cheat sheets, your own plan.

AI Kickstart for everyday life & work

From AI beginner to confident user

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Who are the courses for?

For employees, freelancers, self-employed people, students and anyone who wants to use AI safely in everyday life and at work. Course 01 requires no prior knowledge, course 04 is aimed at advanced users.

When does the next course start?

As soon as enough people have signed up for a course – usually from eight people. We then propose three dates to choose from. Until then, signing up is free and non-binding.

What do the courses cost?

Course 01: €290, course 02: €390, course 03: €490, course 04: €590 – each per person incl. VAT. Packages: Starter-to-Pro €690, Full Learning Pass for all four courses €1,490. Plus 10 % early-bird or group discount; discounts cannot be combined.

Which tools will we learn?

Depending on the course: ChatGPT, Microsoft Copilot, Gemini, Claude, Perplexity, Midjourney, Gamma, Notion AI, Custom GPTs as well as Make and Zapier. The focus is on your tasks, not tool tricks.

Will I get a certificate?

Yes. Everyone receives a certificate of attendance with date, duration and content – for your CV or training record, for example. It is not a certificate from an examination body.
