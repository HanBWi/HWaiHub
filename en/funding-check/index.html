FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is there funding for AI projects in mid-sized companies?

Depending on the German federal state, company size and project, funding options may be worth checking – such as development loans, grants or further-training support. The funding check shows which options you should look into, each with an official source and date.

Does the funding application have to be submitted before the project starts?

For most of the programmes reviewed, yes – but the rules differ. In some cases an awarded contract or a signed agreement already counts as the start. The funding check shows the rule per programme; the official conditions are decisive.

Does go-digital still exist?

No. According to the official notice of the programme administrator, the federal programme go-digital ended on 31 December 2024; no applications can be submitted since 2025. It does not appear as a current option in the funding check.

Is AI or Copilot training eligible for funding?

The further-training support of the German Federal Employment Agency requires, among other things, more than 120 hours and an approved provider – short workshops are generally not covered. Some state and loan programmes take training into account as part of a digitalisation project.

Is the funding check funding advice?

No. It is a first, rule-based orientation without any funding commitment and without legal or funding advice. The binding assessment is made by the responsible funding body.
