  • Connection to ERP, CRM, Microsoft 365 or specialist systems – where needed
  • Key users and training for the teams involved
  • Continue the metrics from the pilot
  • Data rules, approvals and human oversight documented

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Where do we start?

With the free AI Potential Check or an intro call. If you want a sound assessment of your processes, the AI Transformation Analysis is the paid entry point.

What is the difference between the analysis and the pilot?

The analysis clarifies where AI and automation pay off and delivers a cost-benefit calculation and an implementation plan – without implementation. The pilot implements one selected use case in a limited scope and measures the result.

What do the services cost?

The Potential Check and the intro call are free. The AI Transformation Analysis starts from €1,900, the AI & Automation Pilot from €4,900, the AI Partnership from €690 per month. All “from” prices are listed in the price overview on the Services page; we confirm the binding fixed price in writing before we start. Implementation and scaling receive an individual quote. All prices for businesses plus VAT.

Which technologies do you use?

Those that fit the process: Microsoft 365 Copilot, Copilot Studio, Power Automate, Make, AI agents and other AI solutions such as ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini. Sometimes a better workflow without a new tool is the best solution.
