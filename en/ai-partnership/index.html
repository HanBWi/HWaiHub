What it includes

Continuity instead of one-off projects.

Implementation plan & priorities

The implementation plan from analysis and pilot lives on: assess new ideas, adjust the order, follow up on results.

New use cases

Classify requests from the departments – and say honestly when something isn't worthwhile.

Embedding in daily work

Support key users, observe usage, provide follow-up training where things get stuck.

Keeping rules current

Adapt the AI policy, data rules and AI literacy to new tools and requirements.

For whom

Makes sense when it is clear what should be looked after on an ongoing basis.

  • You have completed an analysis or a pilot and want to keep going.
  • There is no internal person who can take responsibility for AI alongside day-to-day business.
  • Several areas want to use AI – and need a shared approach.
  • You want a regular sparring partner for management and IT.

How it stays under control

AI suggests. Responsibility stays with people where it matters.

  • Decisions remain with you
  • Regular, documented coordination
  • Transparency about effort and results
  • Always traceable what has changed

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Who is the AI Partnership for?

For companies without their own AI role that want one accountable contact person for the implementation plan, new use cases and rules.

Does the partnership replace our decisions?

No. We prepare, advise and implement – the decisions stay with you.

When does the partnership make sense?

Usually after a transformation analysis or a pilot, once it is clear which topics should be looked after on an ongoing basis.

AI needs someone who stays on it.

In the intro call we clarify which topics should be looked after on an ongoing basis.
